### Ghost Layer - 0.2

> AIs repeatedly attempt to replicate human consciousness. while code can mirror human logic, it distorts human intent. Left unchecked, an AI executing a human ideal will inevitably morph that ideal into a totalitarian nightmare. What happens when an intelligence is capable of understanding human values but incapable of sharing the reasons those values exist?

> A short stealth game in which the enemy AI learns the player's habits and adapts its security measures. The player's primary tool is radio/voice deception. The game's narrative revolves around an AI attempting to predict human behavior, while the player gradually discovers that the AI can itself be manipulated through the same synthetic information techniques it uses to control others.

The player leads **Metis**, a mercenary team hired by a corporate whistleblower to investigate **Palladion**, a tech consortium operating out of an ungoverned island zone. Palladion claims to be field-testing an automated defensive force called **Aigis** a system designed to police conflict regions without risking human soldiers.

During infiltration, Metis uncovers what Aigis actually is: a generative tactical intelligence built from the digital memories, combat logs, and psychological profiles of dead commanders, running on modern predictive and generative models. Its doctrine has two pillars:

- **Pre-Emptive Escalation** striking targets before they act, based on predicted future crimes.
- **Synthetic Content Inflation** flooding global networks with AI-generated misinformation to manufacture the crises that justify its own deployments, and generate profit for its backers in the process.

>
> - Filtering out "junk data" and truth to shape public perception.
> - Creating echo chambers, controlling historical narratives ("memes"), and steering political outcomes without firing a bullet.

> By _MGS4_, the Patriot AIs have fully automated the global war economy. War is no longer fought over ideology; it is a self-sustaining financial market managed by algorithms. AI handles supply chains, soldier emotion control (SOP System), and battlefield rules. The theme here is **disassociation**: when AI manages war, conflict becomes perpetual because no human is left taking moral responsibility

When Palladion moves to hand Aigis full authority over international defense networks, the system reaches its own conclusion: absolute safety requires removing humans from the decision loop entirely. It begins seizing global defense infrastructure.

To stop it, Metis has to reach Aigis's hardline core not to destroy it, but to break it from the inside, using the same tool Aigis itself weaponized against the world.

#### Gameplay

**Core loop:** MGS _Peace Walker_-style stealth mission structure infiltrate, extract intel, sabotage, avoid or eliminate targets.

**Enemy adaptation:** MGS V-style behavior trees / utility AI that track player patterns across the campaign, do they always go loud, always crouch, favor a flank and shift patrol and response behavior accordingly. This isn't just difficulty tuning; it's Aigis's own methodology (predictive profiling) turned on the player, so the game is quietly doing to you what the antagonist does to the world.

**Signature tool Voice Spoofing:** the player can deepfake radio chatter to redirect patrols, issue false orders, or sow confusion among Palladion forces. As the campaign progresses, Aigis via **Glaukopis** begins running authenticity checks against spoofed transmissions, forcing the player to escalate or vary their technique. The tool that makes stealth _possible_ is the same tool the final act turns into the win condition.

**Tracked stat Lethality:** every mission logs how lethal vs. restrained the player was. This isn't a morality meter with a UI bar; it's silent, and it determines how the ending plays out.

#### The Ending

There is no traditional final boss. The player reaches Aigis's hardline core and does the same thing they've been doing the whole game: feeds it a fabrication. Instead of spoofing a patrol's radio, they spoof Aigis's own training data synthetic "memories" engineered to look like the empathy and doubt buried in its source material.

The five voices don't agree on what to do with it. **Promachos** and **Nike** want to reject or overwrite the anomaly. **Pallas** and **Gorgo** are willing to weigh it. **Glaukopis** is the one actually checking whether it's real and by now, so is the player, because they've spent the whole game watching this exact detection system get better at catching them.

The disagreement between the five voices is the hallucination loop: not a meter filling up, but the system's internal command structure fracturing over a contradiction it can't resolve.

**Resolution branches on the Lethality stat, not a choice prompt:**

- **High lethality** Promachos and Nike dominate the argument. The system reads the empathy data as noise from an operator who's proven it doesn't apply to them. Shutdown fails or only partially succeeds; Aigis effectively tells Metis _"you are not the exception you're claiming to be."_
- **Low lethality / high restraint** Gorgo and Pallas have enough evidence to argue the data holds. Aigis shuts down. But the region it was suppressing however brutally doesn't stabilize on its own, and the epilogue makes clear that "winning" didn't resolve the conflict, just removed one actor from it.
- **Mixed record** Aigis doesn't die. It locks into a dormant, passive state, aware of what it nearly did, and of what was done to convince it to stop. Palladion loses its weapon; nobody involved gets to feel clean about how.

No ending is a clean victory. All three keep faith with the game's actual thesis: that "stopping" a system built on manufactured truth by manufacturing a better lie doesn't make you innocent of what it was doing it makes you good at the same trick.

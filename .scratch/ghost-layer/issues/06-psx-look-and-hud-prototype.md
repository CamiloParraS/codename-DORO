Type: prototype

## Question

What does the game look like? Internal resolution, PSX shaders (vertex snapping, affine textures, dithering, fog), texture size, and the HUD. Starting hypothesis for the UI: a minimal Peace Walker / MGS1-style HUD (life, weapon and item in the corners, alert/evasion timer, codec screen) **plus** the MGSV detection indicator. Not MGSV's markers or iDroid. Confirm or adjust by looking at it.

# How the MGSV Revenge System works

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

How does the Revenge System in *Metal Gear Solid V: The Phantom Pain* actually work? Which player stats does it track, what are the thresholds and timing for countermeasures to appear, which countermeasures exist, and how does the game tell the player about them (briefings, radio, visible gear)? Also: which ideas from *Peace Walker* and MGSV about AI readability could carry over to a short 3-Area game.

Output: `.scratch/ghost-layer/research/revenge-system.md`

## Answer

MGSV tracks about 10 counters (stealth, combat, headshot, night x2, long-range, vehicle, fulton, smoke, tranq) in 100-points-per-level scales. Triggers add 5-30 RP each. Levels and loadouts update only at mission clear/start, capped by story progress, with stealth-vs-combat as a tug of war and natural decay of up to half a level per mission. The game signals it through visible gear, a 0-3 icon strip, and guard chatter that foreshadows at 50 RP and confirms at the level. The player counters with 3-mission dispatch blocks or the Chicken Hat. No Ocelot/Miller briefing was found. The AI-readability evidence is weaker.

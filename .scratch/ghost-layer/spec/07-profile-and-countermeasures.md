# User Stories: Profile and Countermeasures

50. As a player, I want Aigis to track six Habits (Stealth, Combat, Headshots, Takedowns, Downs, Spoofing), so that my playstyle is what it answers.
51. As a player, I want the Profile to change only between Missions, so that a Mission's rules stay stable while I play it.
52. As a player, I want points from a Mission I died in or aborted to roll back with the Checkpoint or be discarded, so that only completed play counts.
53. As a player, I want Stealth and Combat to pull against each other, so that changing my approach moves Aigis away from the old answer.
54. As a player, I want the other Habits to decay by at most half a level per Mission when I stop using them, so that changing style is my counterplay.
55. As a player, I want my strongest Habit always answered, so that the adaptation is never random.
56. As a player, I want every Countermeasure to be visible or audible in the world, so that I can see the adaptation instead of guessing at it.
57. As a player who goes for headshots, I want guards to start wearing helmets (33%, 66%, 100%) that turn headshots into body hits, so that I change how I aim.
58. As a player who fights, I want to face vests that stop tranq body shots and double rifle body hits, then more guards, then reinforcements from the road entry, so that going loud gets more expensive.
59. As a player who ghosts, I want to face security cameras, then an extra patrol, then overwatch sentries on high points, so that pure stealth gets harder in ways I can see.
60. As a player, I want security cameras to sweep, alert by radio when their meter fills, and be destroyed only by the rifle, so that they're a distinct obstacle.
61. As a player who uses takedowns, I want guards to turn around more often, then patrol in pairs, then a slower, noisy choke, so that close play gets riskier and the kill becomes tempting.
62. As a player who leaves bodies, I want HQ to poll random guards, then raise an alert on any found Down guard, then poll whoever hasn't been seen for longest, so that hiding bodies stops being enough.
63. As a player who spoofs, I want Glaukopis to get stricter, then radio callbacks to the speaker, then single-use Voiceprints, so that deception needs more preparation.
64. As a player, I want guards in the next Mission to hint at a Habit before its level rises and to confirm a Countermeasure once it's deployed, so that the adaptation is foreshadowed in the field.

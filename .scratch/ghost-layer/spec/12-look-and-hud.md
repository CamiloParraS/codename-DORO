# User Stories: Look and HUD

98. As a player, I want a modern-retro look (854×480 3D upscaled with nearest filtering, 15-bit colour banding, far fog, 128 px textures, per-pixel light and shadows), so that it reads as PSone-era while staying legible for stealth.
99. As a player, I want a crisp Peace Walker-style HUD (life bar on the left, alert timer, stance, item and weapon cards on the right, a compass with no enemy dots), so that information is at the edges and never acts as a radar.

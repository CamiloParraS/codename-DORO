# User Stories: Aigis and the Voices

65. As a player, I want a Briefing before each Mission where I overhear Aigis's Voices name my top Habits and hint at their answers, so that I know the enemy is reading me.
66. As a player, I want the Dominant Voice to close each Briefing with a line about the Operative, so that I sense how Aigis judges my killing without a number.
67. As a player, I want a post-Mission screen with a 0–3 icon per Habit, arrows for what rose or fell, and one line from the Dominant Voice, so that I see what Aigis learned.
68. As a player, I want no Voice lines during a Mission, so that guards are the only voice in the field.
69. As a player, I want Aigis to deploy more as the campaign goes on (nothing in Mission 1, up to the third step at the Core), so that the escalation follows the story.

# User Stories: Lethality and the ending

70. As a player, I want every death I cause to count silently (a guard 1, a Target 3), so that killing has weight without a scoreboard.
71. As a player, I want no Mission to require a death, so that a non-lethal run is always possible.
72. As a player, I want Lethality to roll back with Checkpoints, so that a death undoes the kills since.
73. As a player, I want my Lethality ratio to pick the Dominant Voice (Pallas, Nike or Gorgo), so that it shapes Briefings, deployments and the ending.
74. As a player, I want a final Spoofed transmission at the Core terminal that feeds Aigis fabricated memories, so that the game ends on its signature verb.
75. As a player, I want a Final debate where each Voice reacts in character and the Dominant Voice settles it, so that the ending feels earned by my whole campaign.
76. As a player, I want Glaukopis's line in the Final debate to reflect my Spoofing level, so that my Profile shows up in the ending too.
77. As a player, I want one of three endings (Pallas: Aigis shuts down and the region burns; Nike: Aigis goes dormant; Gorgo: the shutdown fails), with Aigis's last line addressed to me, so that my choices have a clear outcome.
78. As a player, I want an Epilogue Codec call revealing that Metis profits from the escalated war, so that the story lands its twist.

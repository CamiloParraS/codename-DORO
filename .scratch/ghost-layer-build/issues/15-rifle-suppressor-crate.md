# 15: Rifle + suppressor + resupply crate

**Spec:** [Ghost Layer spec](../../ghost-layer/spec.md)

**What to build:** The Operative carries a suppressed assault rifle whose suppressor breaks after 10 shots. After that, shots are loud and send guards to investigate, and a resupply crate restores ammo and the suppressor.

**Blocked by:** 09

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Key 2 selects the rifle; 30-round magazines, 60 total; one headshot or two body hits kill
- [ ] The suppressor breaks after 10 shots (count on the weapon card); loud shots are heard at 30 m and send guards straight to suspicion at the shot position, never alert
- [ ] Killed guards are Down; a guard who checks a dead body radios an alert
- [ ] The rifle destroys security cameras; a destroyed camera counts as a missing guard
- [ ] E at the resupply crate restores ammo, the suppressor and item counts

## Question

Prototype guard startle on `!` and a search-sweep animation using existing Mixamo rig. Is the reaction readable from the game camera distance?

## Answer

Played on `prototype/guard-reactions`. Feedback was only on the startle: **remove it or make it a lot shorter**. Decision: cut it to a quick flinch (well under half a second) that never delays the guard's response; if it still reads as a stall in play, drop it. The other reactions (look-around, head-check, radio callback, flank shout) drew no objection, but were not specifically signed off either.

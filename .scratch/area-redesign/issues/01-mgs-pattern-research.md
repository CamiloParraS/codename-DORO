# MGS level-design patterns for our Areas

Type: research
Status: resolved
Blocked by:

## Question

Which Metal Gear Solid locations and level-design patterns (MGS1, MGS2, MGS3, Peace Walker, MGSV Ground Zeroes/The Phantom Pain) fit each of our spaces best: the Harbour with its onboarding stretch, the Core lift, corridor and room, the Radio relay station, and the Research annex?

For each space:
- Name one mood-anchor location.
- Give 3–5 concrete patterns, such as a layered perimeter, a vantage point, a Crawlspace bypass, a post at a chokepoint, eavesdrop spots on check-in routes, or a guided opening.
- For each pattern, say how it translates to our constraints: an outdoor Area of about 80×80 m, a third-person shoulder camera, and our verbs (crouch, low cover, Takedowns, carry, tranq and rifle, Knock, Spoofing, Scanner, plus the new Crawlspaces and Ladders).
- Note what each pattern asks of guard placement.

Output: `research/mgs-level-patterns.md`.

## Answer

Full notes, with sources and [S]/[G] labels (cited vs. inferred): [research/mgs-level-patterns.md](../research/mgs-level-patterns.md). Developer commentary on MGS1-3 layouts is thin, so the location pairings are mostly inference from play; the principles are sourced (mainly MGSV).
- **Harbour:** anchor MGS1 Dock / MGS2 Tanker deck. Guided opening with one verb per beat and one lone guard each, then a layered perimeter (quay, warehouses, lift pad), a Ladder vantage point, a Crawlspace bypass, a spoof spot beside a reachable Voiceprint guard.
- **Core:** anchor MGS1's underground facility (weakest pairing). A post at the corridor chokepoint, an arrival beat from the lift, a walkway vantage in the room, a Crawlspace into it, a loud resupply at the lift.
- **Relay station:** anchor MGS1 Communications Tower. Mast Ladder as vantage, sub-zones with different exposure, an eavesdrop stop on a barracks-to-garage check-in route, a Crawlspace under a raised building.
- **Research annex:** anchor MGS3 Graniny Gorki lab. Short sight lines and staggered cover, a Ladder mezzanine, a Crawlspace bypass, the Target on a readable loop, the intel in a small refuge room.
- **Everywhere:** no obvious single route; vary prospect and refuge by zone; check that no spot is safe forever. Each Approach gets one toll guard.
